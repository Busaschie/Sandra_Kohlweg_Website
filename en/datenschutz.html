2. General

I process personal data in connection with my website and my work as a recruitment consultant and headhunter.

Processing is carried out in particular in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the supplementary Romanian data protection provisions, in particular Legea nr. 190/2018.

Personal data includes, for example, name, contact details, career history, qualifications, employer, professional interests and any other information that can be attributed to a person.

3. Visiting this website

When this website is accessed, the hosting provider may process technically necessary data, in particular IP address, date and time of access, browser information and pages viewed.

This processing serves the secure and technically reliable provision of the website on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

This website does not use analytics or marketing cookies or any tracking services.

No contact form is used.

4. Contacting me

If you contact me by email or telephone, I process the data you provide in order to handle your enquiry and for further communication.

This may include in particular your name, company, email address, telephone number and the content of your message.

Where the communication serves to initiate or perform a contract, processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. In other cases it is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Headhunting and direct search

As part of my work, I actively search for suitable specialists and managers and approach potential candidates directly.

In this context, the following data in particular may be processed:

name, business contact details, employer, job position, career history, education, qualifications and publicly available professional information.

The data comes in particular from publicly available professional sources such as LinkedIn or Xing, from recommendations or from existing professional contacts.

Processing is carried out for the purpose of identifying and approaching suitable candidates on the basis of my legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Where personal data is not collected directly from the data subject, information is provided in accordance with Art. 14 GDPR, as a rule at the latest at the first contact or within the statutory period.

Data subjects may object to processing based on legitimate interests pursuant to Art. 21 GDPR.

6. Application and candidate data

If you send me a CV, application documents or other professional information, I process these in order to assess and arrange career opportunities.

This may include in particular contact details, CV, career history, education, qualifications, salary expectations, availability and career preferences.

Providing this information is voluntary.

7. Disclosure to potential employers

Personal candidate data is not passed on to a company merely because I have approached a person.

Data is only disclosed to a potential employer or client as part of a specific placement process and where there is a legal basis for this under data protection law.

Only the information required for the respective placement process is disclosed.

My clients are mainly located in Germany and Austria and, where applicable, in other member states of the European Union.

8. Use of AI services

To support my professional work, I sometimes use AI services, in particular ChatGPT by OpenAI and Claude by Anthropic, for example for editing texts, summarising and structuring information.

Where personal data is processed in this context, only the information required for the respective purpose is used. Wherever possible, personal data is reduced or anonymised beforehand.

When such services are used, processing may take place by providers outside Romania or outside the European Economic Area. International transfers are subject to the statutory requirements of Art. 44 et seq. GDPR.

For users in the EEA, OpenAI Ireland Limited is currently responsible at OpenAI; for international transfers, OpenAI cites adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses, among other things. Anthropic operates Claude in the EU via an Irish company, among others, and points out that, depending on the service, affiliated companies in various countries may also have access to customer data.

9. Storage period

Personal data is only stored for as long as is necessary for the respective purpose or as required by statutory retention obligations.

Candidate data in particular is stored for as long as a placement process is ongoing or further suitable career opportunities may arise.

If further storage is not required and there is no legal basis for it, the data is deleted.

10. Recipients

Where necessary for the respective purpose, personal data may be disclosed in particular to the following recipients:

  • potential employers or clients as part of a specific placement process,
  • IT and hosting providers,
  • email and communication services,
  • AI services used by me.

Any further disclosure only takes place where there is a legal basis for it.

11. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you have in particular the right to:

  • access to your personal data,
  • rectification of inaccurate data,
  • erasure of your data,
  • restriction of processing,
  • data portability, where the conditions are met,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests,
  • withdraw any consent given, with effect for the future.

To exercise your rights, you can contact me at sandra@kohlweg.de.

12. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

The authority responsible for my registered office is in particular:

Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal – ANSPDCP, Romania

Pursuant to Art. 77 GDPR, you may also contact the competent data protection supervisory authority of your place of residence or work.

13. Data security

I take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.
